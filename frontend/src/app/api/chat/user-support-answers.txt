######################################################################POWER ISSUES ANSWERS###############################
[TRIGGERS]
Laptop Charging Port Loose or Not Working, loose charging port, charger socket loose, laptop pin issue
[ANSWER]
Troubleshooting steps for Loose Charging Port:

• Inspect laptop charging port pin for physical bends
• Plug charger gently without applying extra pressure
• Connect charger to another wall socket directly
• Check if charging LED light turns on when held at an angle
• If port is broken contact IT support for socket repair



[TRIGGERS]
Charger Adapter Light Blinking or OFF, charger light blinking, adapter LED off, charger brick dead
[ANSWER]
Troubleshooting steps for Charger Adapter Issue:

• Unplug power adapter from wall socket for 2 minutes
• Check power cord connection between adapter and cable
• Reconnect adapter directly to wall outlet without extension
• Inspect cable wire for sharp bends or exposed copper
• If adapter light stays off replace power adapter via IT



[TRIGGERS]
Laptop Power Light Turns On Then Shut Down, power light turns on then off, laptop auto shutdown on power on, laptop fan spins then stops
[ANSWER]
Troubleshooting steps for Instant Shutdown Issue:

• Unplug charger cable from laptop completely
• Disconnect all external USB drives and mouse dongles
• Press and hold power button down for 30 seconds
• Reconnect charger cable only without USB devices
• Press power button once to check normal boot
• If laptop turns off instantly contact IT for thermal check



[TRIGGERS]
Desktop PC No Power Light at All, desktop no power, cpu dead no light, desktop power button not working
[ANSWER]
Troubleshooting steps for Desktop Power Issue:

• Check CPU power cable behind cabinet is plugged in tightly
• Switch off main wall socket power switch
• Unplug power cable from CPU cabinet back
• Press and hold CPU front power button for 30 seconds
• Plug power cable back firmly and switch on main socket
• If CPU fan does not spin contact IT for power supply check



[TRIGGERS]
Laptop Plugged In But Battery Not Charging, plugged in not charging, battery percentage stuck, battery not increasing
[ANSWER]
Troubleshooting steps for Plugged In Not Charging Issue:

• Press Win + R on keyboard
• Type devmgmt.msc and press Enter
• Expand Batteries section dropdown
• Right click Microsoft ACPI Control Method Battery
• Click Uninstall device option
• Shut down laptop completely from Start menu
• Unplug charger cable and hold power button for 20 seconds
• Reconnect charger and power on laptop to reload driver
